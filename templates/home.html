Welcome to Blood Donation Camp

Your contribution can save lives. Join us in making a difference.

Why donate blood?

Donating blood is a simple, safe way to save lives. A single donation can help several patients who need a transfusion after surgery, an accident or childbirth.

How to donate

Register on the site, then visit the Dhaka North or Dhaka South branch to give blood. Your donation is added to the branch stock the same day.

Be a volunteer donor

Turn on volunteer status in your profile and people searching for your blood group in your zone will be able to reach you directly in an emergency.
