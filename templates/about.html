About our blood donation service

Blood Donation Camp connects donors, recipients and branch blood banks so that a request for blood can be matched against real stock in seconds instead of phone calls.

How the system is organised

Donors

Registered members who opt in to the volunteer list. Their blood group, zone and contact details are shown to people searching for a match.

Recipients

Anyone with an account can search a zone for a blood group and see immediately whether the branch holds enough bags.

Blood banks

Each branch keeps a live count of bags per blood group. Branch managers record every collection and issue.

Blood bag ledger

Every stock movement is written to an append-only ledger, so the current stock of a branch can always be traced back to the records that produced it.
